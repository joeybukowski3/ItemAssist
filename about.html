The replacement problem

Why Accurate Contents Research Requires More Than a Quick Search

Claimed products are often incomplete or difficult to compare. The original item may be:

  • Discontinued or replaced by a newer product generation
  • Partially identified or missing model information
  • Difficult to compare across current product lines
  • Visually similar to products that differ materially in value

Item Assist evaluates the specifications that influence replacement value instead of relying on the first visually similar product or a broad category average.

The research method

How Accuracy Is Built Into the Review

Each accepted item moves through the same disciplined research sequence, from organizing the available evidence to documenting the final comparison.

  1. 1

    Organize the Submitted Information

    • Descriptions and photos
    • Receipts and item lists
    • Brand, model, and serial details
  2. 2

    Identify the Original Item and Age

    • Brand, model, and serial information
    • Manufacture, release, or receipt evidence
    • Verified age or supported estimate
    • Age confidence and missing information
  3. 3

    Determine Value-Relevant Specifications

    • Size, capacity, and materials
    • Performance and features
    • Product tier and generation
  4. 4

    Research Current Replacements

    • Compare products and availability
    • Document current retail pricing
    • Select the most supportable replacement
  5. 5

    Review and Document the Result

    • Replacement reasoning and sources
    • Assumptions and confidence notes
    • Practical limitations
Documented support

More Than a Price
