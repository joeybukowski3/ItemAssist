Household Contents Pricing and LKQ Research

Item Assist provides accuracy-first, item-specific market-pricing research for insurance adjusters, independent adjusting firms, restoration companies, and contents vendors.

We identify the original household item, compare the specifications that affect value, research a supportable current replacement, and document the result with current retail sources, assumptions, and confidence notes.

Accuracy-First Market-Pricing Research

Fast research should still account for the details that make one product materially different from another. Every accepted item is researched individually and human-reviewed before delivery.

Precise Item Identification

Available brand, model, age, specifications, photographs, descriptions, receipts, and supporting documents are reviewed to understand the actual item before a replacement is selected.

Current-Market Replacement Research

Current retail products and documented sources are compared against the original item’s relevant size, capacity, materials, performance, features, generation, model tier, and availability.

Clear Replacement Reasoning

The report documents what was researched, the selected comparison, the current price, source links, required assumptions, and confidence notes for claim-file review.

How It Works

From Available Information to a Documented Report

  1. 1
    Submit the Available Information
    Provide spreadsheets, PDFs, photographs, receipts, model numbers, serial numbers, or written descriptions.
  2. 2
    Scope Is Reviewed
    Item Assist confirms whether the items fit the standard report, identifies missing information, and approves any additional pricing before research begins.
  3. 3
    Research Is Completed
    Each accepted item is researched individually, including the original item, important specifications, potential replacements, current pricing, and supporting sources.
  4. 4
    Receive the Report
    Receive a documented PDF report, with Excel available when appropriate, showing the selected replacement, price, sources, assumptions, and confidence notes.

Standard Item and Specialty Scope

Standard Household Item

A commonly available household product that can reasonably be researched using the submitted description, photographs, receipts, brand, model number, serial number, or other available information.

Standard examples include televisions, computers, tablets, audio equipment, gaming systems, cameras, household appliances, standard furniture, power tools, lawn equipment, and common household goods.

Outside the Standard Launch Scope

Antiques, fine art, jewelry, collectibles, rare instruments, custom-built property, luxury fashion, and items requiring authentication or formal appraisal are not core launch services.

Specialty or unusual items may be reviewed separately, quoted individually, declined, or referred to an appropriate specialist.

Turnaround and Review Capacity

Standard assignments are generally completed within 24–48 business hours after Item Assist receives the information required to begin. Assignments are handled on a first-come, first-served basis.

Turnaround may vary based on file size, item complexity, missing information, specialty research, and current assignment volume. Rush work is considered separately and quoted only when capacity allows.

Clear Role in the Claim Process

Item Assist provides product-identification, replacement, and market-pricing research. Item Assist does not make coverage decisions, interpret policy language, determine depreciation, negotiate settlements, approve payments, establish claim authority, or replace adjuster judgment.

All coverage, depreciation, settlement, and claim-handling decisions remain with the client.
