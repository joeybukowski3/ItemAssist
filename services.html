What We Research

Every service is handled by real professionals using live market data — not automated price engines or unverified estimates.

Electronics Pricing Research

Real-time insurance contents pricing for TVs, laptops, phones, tablets, cameras, and home electronics. We source current retail and secondary-market data to produce claim-ready figures.

Item Specification Verification

We cross-reference serial numbers, model years, and manufacturer records to verify the claimed age and exact specifications of each item, ensuring accurate depreciation and ACV calculations.

LKQ Replacement Recommendations

We identify Like Kind and Quality replacement items for discontinued, obscure, or high-value electronics — with detailed sourcing rationale and direct links to current market options.

ACV & RCV Documentation

We produce complete actual cash value and replacement cost value documentation for each researched item, giving your claim file the sourced, defensible data it needs to withstand audit or dispute.

Large Loss Contents Review

For high-volume or complex contents lists with hundreds of line items, we provide structured review and pricing research at scale — organized, prioritized, and formatted for your claims platform.

Xactimate-Ready Report Delivery

How It Works

From list to report in four steps

Our process is designed around your workflow. No special software, no new platforms — just send us the list.
