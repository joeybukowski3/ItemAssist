We define the original item’s relevant replacement parameters first, then compare current market options against those specifications to avoid accidental upgrades, underpriced substitutes, mismatched specs, and weak comparables. The replacement target is fair, supportable, and claim-ready.

Built for the Reality of Contents Claims

Contents pricing is rarely as simple as finding the first similar product online. Items may be discontinued, partially identified, missing serial data, upgraded by modern product lines, or difficult to compare against current replacements.

Item Assist helps reduce that uncertainty by applying a specialist-led research process to each item. We document what is known, identify what is missing, explain the replacement logic, and provide supportable pricing research before settlement decisions are finalized.

When a direct match exists, we verify it. When it does not, we explain the market clearly.

Why Pricing Accuracy Matters

Even small pricing inaccuracies can create meaningful claim impact at scale. A 10–15% pricing variance on a $10,000 contents settlement can represent $1,000–$1,500 in potential overpayment, underpayment, or disputed value.

For appliance, electronics, specialty, and large-loss contents claims, that impact can grow quickly when multiple items require detailed LKQ review.

Item Assist helps reduce that risk by providing specialist-led market pricing research, documented LKQ reasoning, and claim-ready support before settlement decisions are finalized.

Not Right For Every Assignment

Item Assist is not a coverage advisor, policy interpreter, or settlement authority. We do not price specialty antiques, collectibles, or highly subjective custom items where no reliable market comparable exists.

If a claim requires an insurer-accepted formal appraisal, we can note that clearly in our findings and recommend specialty appraisal review.
