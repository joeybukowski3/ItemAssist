How to read this report

Every statement carries its evidence. Item Assist separates what a manufacturer documents from what a third party measured, what a retailer charged on a given day, and what is assumed for illustration.

  • Documented a manufacturer document or the manufacturer’s own site, or a dated retailer page.
  • Third-party a measurement or guideline from a published outside source.
  • Assumed an input chosen only to show the method. No real unit or condition is described.
  • Not determined the evidence does not establish it, and the report does not guess.

Prices. Retailer prices are the selling prices observed on November 9, 2026, during Black Friday and Holiday Deal promotions. They are not regular market prices. Manufacturer list prices are shown separately where an outside source supports them. Retailer crossed-out prices are not treated as regular prices.

ACV. Calculations show every input. Item Assist does not determine depreciation, final ACV, coverage or settlement; those decisions remain with the client.

Case 3: GE dryer age research

GE’s serial number letters repeat every 12 years, so the same two letters can mean a dryer built in 2008 or in 2020. GE’s own manufacture window for this model leaves one reading. No serial number was available for this example, so no unit date and no single ACV is stated.

Verifying the successor

GE publishes 108 specification fields for each model. 101 are identical, including capacity (7.8 cu ft), 12 cycles, 5 heat selections, steam refresh and dewrinkle, sanitize, 4-way venting, and size (39.75 × 28 × 32 inches). Eight differ: door glass wording (“See-Thru Glass” against “See-Thru”), trim and knob finish, two additional-feature listings, the electrical requirement wording (208/240V against 120/240V), and weight (132 against 134 lb net; 142 against 144 lb shipping). No functional difference was found. A GE-issued specification sheet for the successor was not retrieved, so this comparison rests on GE’s published product-page fields. If an installation is involved, confirm the electrical requirement against GE’s installation instructions.

How GE’s date code works

GE serial numbers begin with two letters. The first letter is the month: A January, D February, F March, G April, H May, L June, M July, R August, S September, T October, V November, Z December. The second letter is the year, and GE’s chart repeats each year letter every 12 years. GE’s own example: “GA” means April 2001 or April 2013.

Limits

  • No serial number or condition information was available, and none is invented here.
  • The window describes the model, not any one unit. A unit can be built anywhere inside it.
  • The date code applies to GE serial numbers in the two-letter, six-digit, one-letter format.
  • The original model’s current availability is unconfirmed, and the successor is the replacement used.

Sources and limits of this sample

  • Prices were observed on November 9, 2026 and will change. They come from sale events and are not regular prices.
  • Replacements were searched within the original’s brand. Cross-brand options were not researched.
  • Item Assist does not determine coverage, depreciation schedules, claim value or settlement.
