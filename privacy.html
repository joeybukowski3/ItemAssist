Privacy Policy

How Item Assist collects, uses, and handles research requests, contact messages, and related information.

Effective Date

Updated November 10, 2026 to describe the research workspace, the contact form, usage limits, spam protection, and website analytics. Originally effective May 4, 2026.

Information We Collect

When you use the research workspace or send a message through the contact form, we may collect the text you type into the workspace; your name, email address, and inquiry type; an optional company name, phone number, and brief description; a random identifier created by your browser; and your IP address. The website does not ask you to upload files, create an account, or pay.

Research Workspace

Text you enter into the research workspace is sent from your browser to Item Assist’s servers and on to the research service behind it, which uses AI and web-search service providers to produce an answer. Please do not enter personal, financial, or other sensitive information. Those providers handle the text you enter under their own terms and privacy policies, which we do not control, and they may retain it. Item Assist’s own services may also keep entered text and answers in logs or caches so the service can operate, avoid repeating identical research, and be kept secure; the website itself does not store your research history.

The workspace applies daily and short-term usage limits. To do that it uses your IP address and a random identifier that your browser keeps in its local storage. The identifier is not tied to your name or email address, and you can clear it by clearing your browser’s site data.

Contact Form

The contact form (“Ask a question or request human-reviewed research”) asks for your name, email address, and the reason for your inquiry (human-reviewed research, additional or paid access, or a general question). It also accepts an optional company name, phone number, and brief description. We use the details to reply to you and, if you ask for human-reviewed research, to discuss what you need.

The form sends your message to Item Assist as an email. The website does not save the message in a separate database, but the email itself is kept in Item Assist’s mailbox and may be recorded in the delivery records of our email service provider.

Campaign Source Tags

If you arrive from a link that carries a short campaign tag in its address (such as utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, or ref), that tag is kept in your browser for the length of your visit and is sent along with a contact message, so we can see which link brought you to the site. We do not collect other tracking information through the contact form. Separately, this website uses Google Analytics, described under Website Analytics below.

Spam and Abuse Protection

Our forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from automated software. Turnstile loads when the homepage opens, runs in your browser and is operated by Cloudflare under its own terms and privacy policy, so Cloudflare may receive your IP address and browser details. The contact form also uses your IP address to count recent requests from the same network address and limit repeated submissions, and uses a random identifier created by your browser, stored only in hashed form, so the same message is not sent twice.

How We Use Information

We use this information to provide and secure the research workspace, apply usage limits, respond to messages, and perform and follow up on human-reviewed research when you ask for it.

Website Analytics

Some pages of this website, such as this policy, use Google Analytics, a service provided by Google, to help us understand website traffic and how visitors interact with pages and links. The research workspace on the homepage does not load Google Analytics.

When Google Analytics is active, your browser sends information to Google that may include the pages you view, the links you click, when and for how long you visit, the page or link that referred you, any campaign tag in the page address, your approximate location, your device, browser, and operating system, and your IP address. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers to recognize your browser across visits.

This information is collected and processed by Google, a third party, under Google’s own terms and privacy policy, which we do not control. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy. You can limit or block cookies in your browser settings, which may affect how analytics works.

Information Sharing

Item Assist does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only as needed to operate the website and research workspace, process form submissions, prevent spam and abuse, measure website traffic, or deliver requested services through trusted service providers that support hosting, email, AI and search research, spam prevention, or analytics. Pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page opens.

Data Retention

We retain submitted information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, complete requested work, operate and secure the research workspace, maintain business records, or comply with legal obligations.

Your Choices

If you need to update submitted contact information or have questions about how your information is being used, contact us at submissions@itemassist.com.

Contact

Privacy questions can be directed to submissions@itemassist.com.
