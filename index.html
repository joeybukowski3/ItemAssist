Similar Isn’t the Same as Comparable

A search for the same brand, size, or category can return products that differ in features, tier, and specifications. Item Assist researches the original item first, then identifies which differences matter before recommending a supportable replacement.

Effect on ACVTaken as a build year, 2025 would mean 0.9 – 1.9 years and an illustrative ACV of $771 – $840. GE’s window supports $419 – $678. Both are illustrative: $899.00, 13-year straight line, no condition adjustment.

Why a range? GE’s serial letters repeat every 12 years, so GE’s manufacture window for the model does the work. With no serial number, the result is an age range (3.2 – 6.9 years) and an illustrative ACV range of $419 – $678, not a single value; a nameplate serial would name one month.
