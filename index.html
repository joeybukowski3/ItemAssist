FAQ

Common questions from adjusters and claims teams

The service is designed to support file quality, consistency, and defensible pricing documentation.

What types of contents items do you research?

We handle standard residential contents, common consumer goods, electronics, furnishings, appliances, and more specialized items when the available documentation supports research development.

How do you determine the appropriate pricing tier?

Tier placement is based on item count, documentation quality, and how much research development is required to reach a defensible result.

What is included in the formal report?

Reports are built around original market pricing research, LKQ replacement findings, age-verification support where available, and a clean format suitable for internal claim review.

Do you provide ACV and RCV support?

Yes. The reporting is prepared to support ACV/RCV analysis by providing the pricing and age-development information adjusters typically need for review.

What if my file has incomplete item information?

We can often work through moderate gaps. Files requiring deeper scope development, specialty research, or consultation may be placed into a higher service tier.

Do you handle high-volume or recurring assignments?

Yes. Enterprise service levels are available for carriers, firms, CAT support, and recurring volume arrangements.

How quickly can reports be delivered?

Standard turnaround ranges from 24 hours to 2 business days depending on service level and research complexity. Enterprise timelines are handled through custom scheduling and SLA discussion.
