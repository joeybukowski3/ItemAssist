Similar Isn’t the Same as Comparable

A quick search can return dozens of products with the same brand, size, or category — but those products can differ by hundreds of dollars because of features, performance, model tier, or specifications that aren’t obvious from the search result. Item Assist researches the original item and identifies which differences actually matter before recommending a supportable current replacement.

  • Televisions: Same brand and screen size can span very different product tiers and prices.
  • Refrigerators: Similar dimensions and capacity do not necessarily mean equivalent features or value.
  • AV equipment: Older discontinued receivers or speakers may require feature-level research to determine a reasonable modern comparable.

Item-Specific Research

We evaluate the available brand, model, age evidence, specifications, photographs, descriptions, and supporting documentation instead of placing the item into a broad pricing category.

Current-Market Sources

Research is based on current replacement products and documented retail sources rather than unexplained pricing buckets, outdated reference books, or unsupported automated estimates.

Clear Replacement Reasoning

Item Assist helps identify the most supportable comparable replacement and shows the reasoning behind it.

Better Pricing Helps Prevent Two Expensive Outcomes

An inaccurate replacement can push a claim above the appropriate current replacement or select a product that does not reasonably match the original item.

1

Pricing Above the Appropriate Replacement

A visually similar product may sit in a different size, performance, feature, or model tier and produce an unsupported higher price.

2

A Replacement That Does Not Reasonably Match

A weak comparison can lead to questions, disputes, and additional customer-service work when the documented specifications do not align.

Item Assist helps reduce both risks by documenting the original item, the selected replacement, the current price, and the reasoning behind the comparison.

Why Item Age Matters

Appliances, electronics, and other rapidly changing products may depreciate significantly as they age. Even a small age difference can materially affect the depreciation information used in an ACV calculation.

Item Assist documents the best-supported age and the evidence behind it so the client can apply its own depreciation methodology with greater confidence.

What a Standard Report May Include

  • Item identification and organization
  • Brand and model research when information is available
  • Verified item age or best-supported estimate when supportable
  • Manufacture-date, model-year, serial, or release-history evidence
  • Age confidence notes and relevant product-generation context
  • Relevant original specifications
  • Current-market replacement research
  • LKQ replacement reasoning
  • Current replacement price
  • Retail source links
  • Research notes
  • Documented assumptions
  • Confidence level
  • PDF report
  • Excel output when appropriate
  • One consolidated round of questions or corrections

Exact manufacture or purchase dates may not always be available. When age evidence is incomplete, the report identifies the result as an estimate and documents the supporting evidence and assumptions. Market prices and retailer availability may change after the report date.
