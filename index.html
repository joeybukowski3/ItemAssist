Comprehensive Contents Research

We handle the tedious pricing work so you can focus on adjusting the claim. Every item is researched by real professionals, not just automated bots.

LKQ Determinations

We locate Like Kind and Quality replacements for discontinued or obscure items, providing detailed reasoning and direct links to current market options.

Age & Condition Verification

We cross-reference serial numbers, model years, and manufacturing data to verify the claimed age of items, ensuring accurate depreciation calculations.

Ready-to-Import Reports

Receive your data in formal PDF reports for the file, plus Excel formats ready to be imported directly into Xactimate or your preferred estimating platform.

A streamlined process for busy adjusters

We integrate seamlessly into your workflow. Just send us the insured’s list, and we handle the rest.

  1. 1
    Submit Your Contents List
    Upload the insured’s handwritten notes, Excel sheet, or PDF via our secure submission form. We accept any standard format.
  2. 2
    We Research Each Item
    Our team identifies exact matches or LKQ equivalents, verifies pricing across multiple retailers, and documents sources with age and condition factors.
  3. 3
    Receive Your Audited Report
    Within 48–72 hours, receive a comprehensive, defensible report with sourced pricing, ACV/RCV determinations, and findings ready for your claim file.
