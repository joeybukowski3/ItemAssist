Frequently Asked Questions

How do you source your pricing data?

We utilize a proprietary blend of verified retail databases, auction house records for high-value items, and direct manufacturer inquiries to establish accurate replacement cost value (RCV).

What is your typical turnaround time?

Turnaround depends on service level and file complexity, with standard reporting beginning at 48 hours and priority service available for time-sensitive assignments.

Are your reports legally defensible?

Yes. ItemAssist reporting is built around documented sourcing, analyst review, and a methodology designed to withstand insurance audits and formal scrutiny.
