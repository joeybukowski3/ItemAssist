Similar Isn’t the Same as Comparable

A search for the same brand, size, or category can return products that differ by hundreds of dollars once features, model tier, and specifications are compared. Item Assist researches the original item first, then identifies which differences actually matter before recommending a supportable current replacement.

Refrigerators

Three similar Samsung French-door models. The example assumes an original French-door refrigerator with dual ice and a beverage center, but no convertible drawer or premium 4-door design.

  • Lower-priced alternative Samsung RF32CG5100SR/AA 3-door, dual ice, smart · Best Buy $1,699.99
  • Selected comparison example Samsung RF32CG5300SR Adds a beverage center · Lowe’s $1,799.99
  • Higher-priced alternative Samsung Bespoke RF29BB8600QL 4-door, FlexZone, Bespoke tier · Lowe’s $2,299.99

A Poor Comparison Can Miss in Either Direction

The replacement figure is only as sound as the comparison behind it.

1

A Figure Higher Than the Original Warrants

A visually similar product from a higher feature or model tier can make the replacement figure unnecessarily high — and harder to support if it is questioned.

2

A Lower-Priced Item That Isn’t Truly Comparable

A cheaper product that lacks features of the original may look reasonable, yet leave the replacement inadequately comparable — a common source of questions and disputes.

Item Assist documents the original item, the selected replacement, the dated price, and the reasoning behind the comparison, so it can be reviewed rather than taken on trust.

Why Item Age Matters

Appliances, electronics, and other rapidly changing products may depreciate significantly as they age. Even a small age difference can materially affect the depreciation information used in an ACV calculation.

Item Assist documents the best-supported age and the evidence behind it so the client can apply its own depreciation methodology with greater confidence.

Clear Service Boundaries

Item Assist provides product-identification, age-evidence, replacement, and market-pricing research. Item Assist does not select depreciation schedules, apply policy provisions, determine depreciation or final ACV, negotiate settlements, approve payments, establish claim authority, or replace adjuster judgment.

All coverage, depreciation, settlement, and claim-handling decisions remain with the client.
