    What to Expect

    Accuracy-First, Human-Reviewed Research

    Human-Reviewed Findings

    Model and serial evidence is reviewed by a person before delivery, not just returned from an automated lookup.

    Source Documentation

    Findings reference the model records, release history, and public sources used to reach a conclusion.

    Clear Limitations

    When evidence is incomplete, the report says so rather than overstating confidence.

    Typical 24–48 Hour Turnaround

    After scope and authorization are confirmed. Rush service may be available by request. Covers household appliances, electronics, and everyday contents — not formal appraisal or onsite diagnostic work.
